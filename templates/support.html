❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is NotHere.one?

NotHere.one is an ethical search engine — an "algorithm of justice" that filters out harmful content while boosting diverse and trustworthy voices. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

How is content filtered?

We exclude content from SPLC-designated hate groups, misinformation and conspiracy sites, adult content, gambling, predatory lending, and MLM schemes. We boost minority-, women-, veteran-, LGBTQ+-, and disability-owned businesses along with B-Corp certified companies. The full breakdown lives on our privacy page.

How do I report a result?

Every result has 👍 / 👎 feedback buttons — that's the fastest signal and it feeds straight into our scoring. For something more serious (a site that should be filtered, or one we filtered by mistake), email support@nothere.one with the URL and the search that surfaced it.

When will I hear back?

We're a small team, so expect a reply within a few business days. Reports about harmful content that slipped through the filter jump the queue.
