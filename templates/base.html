About NotHere.one

🔍 What is NotHere.one?

NotHere.one is an ethical search engine — an "algorithm of justice" designed to filter harmful content while amplifying diverse and trustworthy voices.

🛡️ What We Filter Out

  • Hate groups flagged by SPLC and civil rights organizations
  • Misinformation and conspiracy content
  • Adult content, gambling, and predatory lending
  • MLM schemes and content farms

✊ What We Boost

  • Minority-owned businesses
  • Women-owned businesses
  • Veteran-owned businesses
  • LGBTQ+-owned businesses
  • Disability-owned businesses
  • B-Corp certified companies

🧠 Internet Points

When you engage with our media literacy features — fact-checking claims and exploring counter-evidence — you earn Internet Points! It's our way of rewarding critical thinking.
